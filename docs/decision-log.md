# Decision log

One entry per decision that changes the specification or the way of
working: decision, who decided, why, and where it is written down.
The entries are listed in the order they were made; each is numbered
within its kind, and a later entry that supersedes or confirms an
earlier one names it by its number:

- **P**: project and way of working
- **E**: ethics and safety
- **U**: interface and accessibility
- **A**: architecture, code and hosting
- **M**: models and AI
- **D**: data and catalogue
- **R**: roadmap

The log is also the evidence, required by the HackYeah rules, that the
core idea and the decisions are the team's own work. AI assistants append
entries at the end for decisions people make, with the next number of
the kind; they never decide.
